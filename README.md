# climate-learn

repo for climate and energy issue learning.

Working notes and literature briefs, built from primary sources — treaty text, UNFCCC decisions,
national regulations and statistics — with every claim traceable to its document.

**Published site:** https://cidds.github.io/climate-learn/

**Single-page version:** [carbon-economic-mechanisms/all-in-one.html](carbon-economic-mechanisms/all-in-one.html) — every chapter in one file, works offline.

## Briefs

### [Carbon Pricing](carbon-economic-mechanisms/) — 12 parts

Carbon pricing and carbon markets, globally and in Indonesia. Anchored in **energy** (power, industry,
efficiency) with **forestry** as contrast, and read through three lenses: government, project
developer, project owner.

| | |
|---|---|
| Global foundations | Why carbon pricing exists · UNFCCC–Kyoto–Paris · Article 6 end to end · how a methodology makes a tonne · compliance beyond the ETS · the voluntary market |
| Indonesia | The Second NDC (2025) · Perpres 110/2025 read in full · the institutional map |
| Applied | Energy vs forestry · one project traced gate by gate · what remains unknown |
| Updates | The ministerial implementing regulations, as they land |

Primary documents read include the UNFCCC Convention annexes, Decision -/CMA.3 (the Article 6.4
rulebook), Indonesia's Second NDC, Perpres 110/2025, POJK 14/2023, and the draft Permenhut on
forestry carbon offset trading.

## On sourcing

Every brief carries a **source ledger** distinguishing what was read directly from what is cited by
reference from what is flagged uncertain. Claims declined for want of a source are marked `[?]` in the
text. Corrections are left visible rather than quietly patched — the carbon brief carries one.

## Caveat

Personal learning notes, not legal, policy or investment advice. Some sources are drafts or secondary;
those are flagged in the text. Indonesian regulation was moving while this was written — verify
against the gazette before relying on any article number.
