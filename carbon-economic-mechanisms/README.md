# Carbon Pricing — a literature brief

A twelve-part brief on carbon pricing and carbon markets, globally and in Indonesia, built from primary
sources: treaty text, UNFCCC decisions, Indonesia's Second NDC, Perpres 110/2025, and the ministerial
implementing regulations.

Anchored in **energy** (power, industry, efficiency) with **forestry** as contrast, and read through
three lenses: government, project developer, and project owner.

**Start at `index.html`.** Or read [`all-in-one.html`](all-in-one.html) — every chapter and the source ledger in a single file, with a collapsible chapter menu.

## Structure

| | |
|---|---|
| `00_Orientation/` | Vocabulary — every term given twice, as global concept and Indonesian legal definition |
| `01_Global_Foundations/` | Why carbon pricing exists · the UNFCCC–Kyoto–Paris stack · Article 6 · methodologies · compliance beyond ETS · the voluntary market |
| `02_Indonesia_NEK/` | Indonesia's Second NDC · Perpres 110/2025 read in full · the institutional map |
| `03_Sector_Application/` | Energy vs forestry, tested against the mechanisms |
| `04_Synthesis/` | One project traced end to end, three lenses, and what remains unknown |
| `05_Updates/` | Added after the brief closed, as the regulatory picture moves |
| `_Sources/` | The running source ledger — every claim, its document, and its verification status |

## How to read it

- **Dotted underline** — hover for a definition
- **Footnote marker** — hover for the source, click to jump to the full citation
- **`[?]` in pink** — a claim declined for want of a source. Treat as unverified.
- **Collapsed blocks** — depth kept out of the main flow

## On sourcing

Every claim is logged in `_Sources/source_ledger.html`, which distinguishes what was **read directly**
from what is cited **by reference** from what is **flagged uncertain**.

One correction stands in the set: Session 05's claim that Indonesia's carbon tax caps its allowance
price was wrong, and is corrected in Session 08. It is left visible rather than quietly patched.

Primary documents read: UNFCCC Convention Annexes I & II · Kyoto and Paris Agreement (by article) ·
Decision -/CMA.3 (Article 6.4 rulebook) · Indonesia's Second NDC (2025) · Perpres 110/2025 ·
Permenhut on forestry carbon offset trading (draft) · POJK 14/2023 · Permen ESDM 16/2022 (via ESDM) ·
DJK Statistik Ketenagalistrikan 2024 · PLN RUPTL 2025–2034 (contents only).

## Caveat

This is a personal learning brief, not legal or investment advice. Several sources are drafts or
secondary; those are flagged in the text. Indonesian ministerial regulations were moving while this
was written — verify against the gazette before relying on any article number.
