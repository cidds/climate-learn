Presidential Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia No. 110 of 2025. Replaces Perpres No. 98 of 2021 (stated in the recitals, Menimbang huruf c, p. 1). The governing NEK instrument, 204 pages. Read so far: pp. 1–15 (Session 00 — Pasal 1 definitions, NDC planning, inventory), pp. 38–53 (Session 03 — BAB III trading architecture, Pasal 55–78), pp. 56–64 (Session 03 — validation/verification, registries, SPE-GRK, steering committee). Remaining pages and full structural read scheduled for Session 08.

Decision -/CMA.3 — Rules, modalities and procedures for the Article 6.4 mechanism

UNFCCC, Glasgow 2021 (advance unedited version). The Article 6.4 / PACM rulebook. Read in Session 04: decision paras 1–17 and annex paras 26–75, covering host Party participation, activity design, methodologies, baselines, additionality, the activity cycle, the mechanism registry, share of proceeds, OMGE, corresponding adjustments, CDM transition and CER use.

UNFCCC Convention text (1992) — Annex I and Annex II

The Convention as adopted at New York, 9 May 1992. Read in Session 04, added to Session 02: Annex I (p.32, 36 entries with 11 marked as economies in transition) and Annex II (p.33, 25 entries). The authoritative source for the Annex classification — the lists are annexes to the treaty itself, not a separate publication.
