One correction stands in this set

In Session 05 I said Indonesia's carbon tax acts as a price ceiling on its allowance market. That was wrong — I reasoned from a generic hybrid-instrument model without reading UU HPP. The statute pegs the rate at or above the carbon market price, making it a floor under compliance rather than a ceiling on price, and Indonesia's design stronger than I described.

Session 05 now carries a correction banner and the struck text; Session 08 sets out the full correction and its sourcing. The lesson generalises: reasoning correctly from a generic model is exactly how confident, wrong advice gets produced.
